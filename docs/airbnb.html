Method

Four quarterly Inside Airbnb snapshots of Broward County, June 2024 to 19 March 2025. For each listing the study reads the 365-day calendar after a snapshot: booked nights, the average daily rate (ADR), occupancy, RevPAR (revenue per available night) and expected annual revenue. Headline numbers are medians over entire homes in the latest snapshot (14,701 of 17,237 active listings). Each median is taken on its own, so the median ADR times the median occupancy ($52) is close to, but not the same as, the median RevPAR ($57). A nightly rate counts as a price only from $10 to $2,500 and off the placeholder values hosts type on blocked nights ($999, $1,000, $9,999, $10,000); 939 entire homes fall outside, so their revenue is not measured (their occupancy still is). A neighbourhood is ranked only with at least 50 entire homes. A multi-listing host holds 10 or more active listings.

Calendar availability is a proxy for bookings: a blocked night can be a stay or an owner's hold, so occupancy is an estimate, labelled as such.
